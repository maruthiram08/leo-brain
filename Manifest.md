
# 1) Public Positioning Page (Category-Defining)

This is written as a **homepage + manifesto hybrid**.  
You can ship this almost verbatim.

---

## Hero

### Leo remembers so you don’t have to.

**Save anything, instantly.  
Forget about it completely.  
Trust it will come back when it matters.**

Leo is not a notes app.  
It’s not a productivity system.  
It’s not something you manage.

It’s a quiet layer that remembers what crossed your attention.

---

## The Problem (Call out the pain directly)

### Why do “second brain” apps feel like a second job?

Because they ask you to:

- Decide what something is
    
- Decide where it belongs
    
- Decide how to organize it
    
- Maintain the system forever
    

That’s not memory.  
That’s unpaid admin work.

---

## The Reframe

### Memory doesn’t work like folders.

You don’t remember things because you tagged them.  
You remember things because:

- You saw them again
    
- You needed them
    
- They became relevant
    

Leo is built the same way.

---

## What Leo Is

### An ambient memory layer

Leo quietly watches what passes through your attention:

- A link you saved
    
- A thought you dropped into Telegram
    
- A page you captured with a shortcut
    

You don’t name it.  
You don’t organize it.  
You don’t think about it again.

Leo does.

---

## What Leo Is Not (Important)

Leo is **not**:

- A notes app
    
- A knowledge base
    
- A task manager
    
- A system to maintain
    
- A place to be productive
    

If you’re looking for structure, dashboards, or optimization —  
Leo is the wrong product.

---

## How Leo Works (Simple, Honest)

### 1. Capture without thinking

Send a message. Press a shortcut. That’s it.

### 2. Everything becomes a stream

No inbox. No backlog. Just time.

### 3. Meaning emerges later

Things you revisit matter more.  
Things you never touch quietly fade.

### 4. Relevant things come back

When you search, write, or revisit a topic,  
Leo gently reminds you:

> “You’ve seen this before.”

---

## The Emotional Promise

Leo doesn’t promise:

- More productivity
    
- Better organization
    
- Smarter workflows
    

Leo promises one thing:

### Peace of mind.

You can stop worrying about forgetting.  
You can stop managing information.  
You can trust that what matters will return.

---

## Who Leo Is For

Leo is for people who:

- Think a lot
    
- Read a lot
    
- Capture fragments, not documents
    
- Hate maintaining systems
    
- Want relief, not control
    

Leo is **not** for people who enjoy organizing.

---

## Closing Line

### Save freely. Forget confidently.

Leo remembers.
